# Etsy shop setup

Etsy shops have to be opened by the shop owner at **etsy.com/sell**, which needs an ID, a bank account and tax details. This file gives you everything else ready to paste.

## Shop basics
- **Shop name:** use the same name as the website (Etsy shop names are 4–20 characters with no spaces, for example `TheStitchAtelier`).
- **Shop title:** Couture crochet & hand-knit heirlooms by a retired teacher
- **Announcement:** Every piece is made by hand, in natural fibers, in small numbers. Each month, 10% of every order supports a different nonprofit, including veteran organizations. Made-to-order requests are welcome.
- After opening, paste your shop address into `etsyUrl` in `content/site.js`. The website then links to it.

## About section (Etsy "About" → Story)
> For thirty years I taught in the classroom, helping young people find their words and take pride in careful work. I hold a master's degree in English and Education, and crochet and knitting have been with me the whole way. In retirement, that quiet practice became this small atelier. I make a few beautiful, lasting pieces at a time, in natural fibers, entirely by hand. Each month part of every sale goes to a different nonprofit, with special care for those who serve our veterans. Thank you for supporting slow, handmade work.

## Etsy best practices
- **Photos:** use all 10 slots. Include the main photo on a plain background, someone wearing it, a close-up of the stitches, a size reference and a folded or gift shot. Square crops show best in Etsy search.
- **Keep it curated:** list the same 6–9 pieces as the website, with color options as variations.
- **Pricing:** Etsy charges a $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% on sales and payment processing, so price the Etsy listings to cover them. Keep the website price the same or a little lower to reward direct customers.
- **Processing times:** set ready-to-ship and made-to-order times honestly. On-time shipping affects your search ranking.
- **Reviews:** include a handwritten thank-you card. It's a teacher's touch and it earns reviews.
